Video Editing & AI Video Generation

A hobby I keep learning — storytelling with footage, sound and AI-generated visuals, built in my free time

Video editing is a hobby, not a job title. Nobody assigned it to me — I picked it up because I wanted to understand what makes a video hold someone’s attention, and I have been teaching myself ever since, one video at a time, in the evenings and on weekends when the code is closed for the day.

What keeps me at it is the exploring. Every new tool, transition or AI model is something to pull apart and test on a real video rather than read about, and the feed tells me honestly whether it worked. That habit is the same one I bring to backend work: learn it by building something real with it.

Two channels are where that practice lives. Past Unseen is short-form Nepali history and heritage — I research, script, edit and publish each one myself, using AI video and voice tools to create visuals for stories no camera ever recorded. Bijok Ramailo is the lighter side, a Nepali comedy page that taught me how fast an idea has to land on a scrolling feed. Both are made in free time, for the love of it, and both have made me a sharper storyteller than any tutorial could.

What I can do

The parts of a video I handle end to end

Editing & Story Structure

Cutting raw footage into a sequence that holds attention — hook, build, payoff — with pacing tuned to the platform it is going out on.

Short-Form & Reels

Vertical 9:16 edits for Reels, Shorts and TikTok: punchy cuts, captions, beat-matched transitions and safe-area aware framing.

Motion Graphics & Titles

Lower thirds, animated titles, logo stings, callouts and simple infographic animation to make information readable on screen.

Colour & Finishing

Colour correction and grading for a consistent look across shots, plus export presets per platform so quality survives compression.

Sound Design

Dialogue cleanup, noise reduction, music selection, ducking and sound effects — the half of video most people only notice when it is wrong.

AI Video Generation

Text- and image-to-video generation with Google Flow, Gemini and Grok — shot-by-shot prompting to build scenes that were never filmed, then cut and graded by hand.

My second page

Bijok Ramailo — Comedy Nepal

Nepali comedy · short-form reels

A lighter page built on one simple idea: take an everyday Nepali situation — payday running out, guests at the door, Dashain on a tight budget — and act it out with animals. A dog’s face, a monkey’s reaction or a cat’s timing says the punchline faster than any actor can, and it reads the same to every age group, in any language, with the sound off.

That makes the format work for advertising as well as for laughs. The structure is deliberately repeatable: a hook in the first two seconds, one clear idea, the animal reaction as the punchline, and a clean end frame where a brand, product or call to action can sit without breaking the joke. Shot vertical, captioned in Nepali and English, and cut to the same 15–30 second template every time — so a sponsor gets a predictable, on-brand result instead of a one-off.
