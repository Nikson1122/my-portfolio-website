BusSewa — Online Bus Ticketing Platform

Nepal’s fastest-growing online ticket booking platform.

BusSewa lets passengers across Nepal search routes, pick seats and pay online, issuing an instant digital ticket. The platform spans 400+ routes across 65+ districts, works with 500+ bus partners and 1,700+ daily buses, and serves mobile apps with 500K+ Android downloads. I work on the backend that powers all of it.

  • Backend services & REST APIs consumed by the website and the Android and iOS apps — search, booking, ticketing, cancellation and operator endpoints.
  • Payment gateway integrations across eSewa, Khalti, IME Pay, NCHL connectIPS and MyPay — payment initiation, verification callbacks, idempotent transaction handling, failure recovery and refunds on cancellation.
  • Booking & seat-inventory engine with seat locking and transactional integrity, so concurrent users cannot double-book the same seat on high-demand routes.
  • Route, schedule and fleet modules letting operators manage buses, layouts, fares and departures against a live inventory of 55k+ seats a day.
  • Promo codes and flash-sale logic, including validation rules, usage limits and discount application at checkout.
  • Enterprise web interfaces in JSF for internal and operator-facing administration.
  • Database design and query optimisation for a high-read, booking-heavy workload, plus notification flows for ticket confirmation.
